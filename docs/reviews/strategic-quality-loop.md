## Candidate 2: same-critic reassessment

Fresh captures showed local scope active by default, Forge condition/stock rows and the explicit unavailable-diagnosis note above the fold. Construction showed nine human-labelled completed facilities and zero projects before campaign data. The critic marked the central local-first finding, duplicate action pair, action contrast, narrower workspace and secondary identifiers corrected.

Newly exposed major defects remained blocking acceptance:

- A universal “Selected locality” workspace scope label misrepresented campaign-wide Military and Orders contents. Required correction: explicit campaign data scope plus separate retained selection context, and scope labels tracking actual Local/Campaign toggles.
- At 390×844, Population was clipped to “1,” rather than its full value. Required correction: responsive resource sizing/wrapping and readable whole values.
- Phone breadcrumbs overlapped Map/Overlay controls; zoom, legend and help regions collided. Required correction: separated stacked control regions and actual touch hit-target verification.

Moderate follow-ups: inspector ID/provenance repetition, raw military table readability, menu grouping and ordinary keyboard/dialog focus. Source inspection found request-context guards, per-view scroll, Return-to-map state updates, menu expanded-state and visible focus targets improved, but did not claim an independent browser pass.

The selected-character capture exposed role/assignment/experience and explicitly unavailable relationship records. The critic accepted its honesty while marking the CK3 relationship-comprehension task unavailable due to absent data/mechanics. Role is not treated as a fabricated personal name. The economic diagnosis task remained partial: initial stocks and condition do not establish a ranked main problem or forecast.

## Final candidate: independent disposition

The same critic independently inspected ten fresh full-frame screenshots after the final scope/mobile fixes. It accepted the implemented settlement → local Economy → Construction → map workflow with no unresolved critical or major UI blocker in that scope. Military and Orders now correctly announce campaign-wide content with retained selection. Phone resources show the full Population value (1,200); controls, breadcrumbs, legend and footer no longer collide. Expanded browser results were supplied separately; the critic did not rerun the suite or infer interaction correctness from images.

Task outcomes:

- **Victoria 3 comparison: partial.** Recorded stocks, condition and facilities are clearer and local-first. Identifying an authoritative main economic problem remains unsupported where diagnosis/trend data is absent. No task parity claim.
- **CK3 comparison: unavailable.** Character role/assignment/experience inspection works; relationship understanding is blocked by absent recorded relationships. Explicit unavailability is accepted, not relationship-task completion.
- **Stellaris comparison: accepted within the tested path.** Visible Return to map/Menu/hierarchy controls and browser evidence establish retained camera, overlay, selection and canvas across this management journey. No broader equivalence claim.

Remaining nonblocking findings: raw Military schema tables wrap IDs and can clip rightmost columns; province inspector gives IDs and Unknown province totals too much prominence relative to recorded settlement subsets; mobile scope headings wrap excessively. Minor findings include small captions, duplicate Reach breadcrumb naming in the fixture, truncated campaign dropdown text, technical Construction subtitle, role-based character heading without immediate ID disambiguation, and inherited phone map letterboxing/small targets. Future corrections should prioritize readable report summaries and secondary identifiers without inventing condition or relationship mechanics.
